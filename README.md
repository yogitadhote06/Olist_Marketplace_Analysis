Olist Marketplace Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the performance and customer experience of the "Olist Brazilian e-commerce marketplace" using data from orders, customers, sellers, products, payments, reviews, and logistics.

The analysis aims to identify the key factors influencing customer satisfaction and highlight operational areas where Olist can improve delivery performance, seller performance, and overall customer experience.

🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall marketplace performance and sales trends
* Understand customer satisfaction and review patterns
* Investigate the relationship between delivery delays and customer reviews
* Identify high-performing and underperforming product categories
* Evaluate seller and geographic performance
* Analyze payment behavior
* Identify operational opportunities and provide actionable recommendations

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python**
* **Pandas & NumPy** – Data cleaning and analysis
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization

📁 Dataset

The analysis uses the Olist public e-commerce dataset, containing information about:

* Orders
* Order Items
* Payments
* Reviews
* Customers
* Sellers
* Products
* Geolocation
* Product Category Translation

🔍 Key Analysis Areas

# Customer Experience

Analyzed review scores, satisfaction levels, delivery performance, and the relationship between delays and customer dissatisfaction.

# Delivery Performance

Examined delivery time, late-delivery rates, and delay severity across different periods and locations.

# Seller Performance

Compared sellers based on order volume, revenue, review scores, delivery performance, and customer dissatisfaction.

# Product Categories

Identified high-revenue and high-volume categories and evaluated their customer satisfaction performance.

# Geographic Analysis

Compared customer states based on order volume, delivery time, late-delivery rate, and review scores.

# Payment Analysis

Evaluated customer behavior and satisfaction across different payment methods.

💡 Key Findings

* Overall customer satisfaction is relatively strong, with approximately **76.6% of reviews rated 4–5 stars**.
* Delivery reliability is strongly associated with customer satisfaction.
* Late orders have substantially higher dissatisfaction than orders delivered on time or early.
* More severe delays, particularly **4+ days**, are associated with significantly lower review scores.
* Performance varies considerably across customer states.
* Some high-volume and high-revenue product categories present opportunities for customer-experience improvement.
* Freight burden shows a weaker association with satisfaction compared with delivery performance.

📌 Business Recommendations

1. Establish an early-warning system for severe delivery delays.
2. Prioritize high-volume sellers and logistics routes contributing the largest number of late orders.
3. Set state-level delivery performance targets for underperforming regions.
4. Focus improvement initiatives on high-revenue categories with weaker customer satisfaction.
5. Monitor freight cost burden alongside delivery performance.
6. Build an ongoing customer-experience dashboard for operational monitoring.
